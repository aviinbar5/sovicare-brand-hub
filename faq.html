Getting started
What is SoviCare?+

SoviCare is an online health platform that helps adults explore care for concerns including sexual health, hair loss and wellness. You share your health information from home and a licensed healthcare provider reviews it and decides whether a treatment is appropriate for you.

How does SoviCare work?+

A short private assessment, a licensed provider review, labs where needed, and a plan only if one is appropriate. See How it works.

Providers and prescriptions
Will I need to speak with a provider?+

Not always by phone or video. Your provider decides whether a conversation is needed.

Am I guaranteed a prescription?+

No. A provider prescribes only when it is medically appropriate, and will tell you when the answer is no.

What is a compounded medication?+

A medication prepared by a licensed pharmacy for an individual patient. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. See the Telehealth notice.

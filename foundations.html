Layout
and grid

One page width, one grid, one measure. A section is a full-bleed band of colour with the content held to the wrap; nothing floats outside it.

Spacing

Eight steps off a 48px unit. Nothing below 4px, and no value that is not on this list.

Iconography

Drawn on the same geometry as the Quadrant Kit: a 24px box, 1.5px stroke, round caps and joins, no fill, no two weights in one icon. An icon labels a thing; it never replaces the label.

Photography

Men in their own life, at rest rather than at performance. Available light, no studio gloss, no gym, no clinic. Nothing is looked at alone — people appear in relation.

Motion

Motion confirms, it never performs. Four durations, one easing, one distance. If an animation draws attention to itself it is wrong.

Voice
and tone

The human sentence first, then the precise one. Calm, adult, unembarrassed. We do not sell relief and we do not perform empathy.

application

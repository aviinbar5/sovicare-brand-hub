Understand it first

Labs first, then a plan.

Low energy has more than one possible cause — testosterone, thyroid, ferritin, B12, blood sugar, sleep apnoea. A blood panel narrows it in days rather than years. Your provider reads it with you before anything is recommended.

Questions people ask about this.

Do I need labs before anything is offered?+

Usually yes for longevity care. It is what separates a plan from a guess.

How long until I notice anything?+

It varies by person and by option. Your provider will say what to expect, and when to check in.

What if my results are normal?+

Then that is useful to know. Your provider will say whether anything is worth doing, including nothing.

Is this available everywhere?+

Availability depends on where you live. The assessment will tell you before you share anything sensitive.

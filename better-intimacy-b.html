Understand it first

Three different concerns, often three different causes.

Erection difficulty, lower desire and finishing sooner are not the same thing. Blood flow, hormones, blood pressure, stress, sleep and medication can all play a part. Choosing a product before knowing which applies is why so many men try three things and stop.

That is why a provider looks at the whole picture — labs where needed — before anything is suggested.

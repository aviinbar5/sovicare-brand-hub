The four moves

Every animation above is built from these and nothing else. A motion that can't be described in one of these four words does not belong to SoviCare.

Pivot

A quadrant swings in around its own corner. 90° exactly, never 87°. 820ms.

Wipe

Photography and full-bleed panels arrive as a hard edge travelling across. Never a fade.

Rise

Type only. Line by line, 0.42em, 120ms apart. Words never scale and never rotate.

Shift

A held shape changes colour between brand hues. Slow — 3s per step, never a gradient.

Reduced motion: every animation here is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. The composition has to read as a still frame first.

Screens · Support

Helpful, and deliberately blind.

A support agent can move an order and reset a login, and cannot see a lab value, a diagnosis, or a date of birth. The interface has to make that limit feel like care rather than obstruction.

Rule

The PHI wall

Every hidden field gets the same treatment: a Sand panel, a lock, and a sentence saying who can see it. Never a blank, never a greyed-out box, never an error.

When will my order ship?

Approved orders ship within two business days, and you'll get a tracking number by text and email the moment it leaves the pharmacy. Everything arrives in plain packaging with no branding on the outside.

Why was I charged before anything shipped?

The charge is authorised at checkout and only captured once a clinician approves your request. If the clinician decides treatment isn't appropriate, the authorisation is released and you're not charged at all.

Is this side effect normal? CLINICAL · ESCALATE

Do not answer this yourself. Escalate to the care team and tell the patient a licensed provider will reply — usually within one business day, sooner if they describe anything urgent.

I can't log in — the code never arrives.

I've sent a fresh login code — it should arrive within a minute. If it still doesn't, it's usually the carrier blocking short codes, and we can switch you to email instead.

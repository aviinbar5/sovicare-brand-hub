Andropause

It has a name, and it is not a character flaw.

From around forty, testosterone declines slowly — roughly one per cent a year for most men. Slowly enough that nothing announces itself, and fast enough that a decade later the man in the mirror sleeps worse, recovers slower and cares less.

It is not a diagnosis on its own, and it is not the explanation for everything. Several unrelated things produce the same feeling, which is exactly why we measure before we name anything.

Four things we do not compromise on.

What a clinician actually does with your answers.

Nothing is automated at the point where it matters. A licensed provider reads what you wrote and takes responsibility for the decision.

They read Your assessment answers, your eligibility questionnaire, and anything you wrote in your own words.
They may order Labs, where they are needed to answer the question you came with. Not as a matter of course.
They decide Whether an option may be appropriate for you, based on eligibility — including the option of nothing.
They will not Prescribe from symptoms alone, or continue anything without reviewing how you are actually doing on it.

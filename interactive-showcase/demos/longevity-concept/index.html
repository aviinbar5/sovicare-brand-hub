UNDERSTAND IT FIRST

Good decisions start with realistic expectations.

THE EVIDENCE

Longevity research is evolving. A treatment’s role in a biological process does not, by itself, prove that taking it will improve how you feel, reverse aging, or help you live longer.

YOUR PROVIDER

Your provider can explain the evidence and uncertainties for the specific product being considered.

FOUNDATIONS

The foundations
still matter.

Regular physical activity, adequate sleep, balanced nutrition, and routine medical care remain important parts of healthy aging. Discuss these alongside any treatment you are considering.

QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Will these treatments make me live longer?

These products have not been established as treatments that extend human lifespan. Your provider can discuss the evidence, risks, and realistic expectations for any option under consideration.

Are these options available to both men and women?

SoviCare’s healthy-aging category is designed for adults of all genders. Eligibility for a particular treatment depends on the product, medical history, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, and provider assessment.

Do I need blood tests?

That depends on the treatment and your medical history. Your provider may require testing or recommend further evaluation before prescribing. Any testing requirements and costs will be explained before you proceed.

Can I take more than one product?

Do not combine treatments on your own. Share all medications and supplements with your provider so they can assess interactions and suitability.

Are these treatments FDA approved for anti-aging?

The products listed here are not presented as FDA-approved anti-aging treatments. Approval for one formulation or medical condition does not establish approval for another use. Compounded medications are not FDA approved.

How much is the consultation?

Your consultation is included when you purchase treatment. If you do not purchase treatment, the consultation costs $25. Medication pricing is shown before purchase.

What happens if treatment isn’t appropriate?

Your provider may recommend further evaluation or another approach to care. Completing an assessment or consultation does not guarantee a prescription.

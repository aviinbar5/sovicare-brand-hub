06 / GOOD QUESTIONS WELCOME

Good to ask.

Sometimes the first useful step is simply knowing what to expect.

Where do I start?+

Start with the area that feels most relevant, or with a private assessment. The final service journey and wording will be confirmed before launch.

Do I need to know which treatment I want?+

No. Begin with what you have noticed. Your answers can help a provider understand your circumstances and consider appropriate options.

Who reviews my information?+

A licensed provider reviews the assessment before any treatment is offered. The exact process will be checked against the final product and legal copy.

Hub Foundations UX Patterns

Buttons

Three, and only three. All fully round, 46px high, Inter 500 at 14px. Copy stays identical through the flow.

Answer
options

Four shapes for one job. Which one to use is decided by the answer set, not by the designer: six or more short labels take cards, four to five sentences take rows, an ordered range takes the scale, and a two- or three-way take the wide row.

Fields

Square-cornered on a 14px radius, 1px Line at rest, Blue on focus, Coral on error. The label sits above and never becomes a placeholder.

What the full sequence reveals. Nothing has been removed. These are the observations that only became visible once all sixteen sections sat in order, recorded for your decision after review.
1The 10–13 trough Four consecutive low-intent sections — social proof, supplements, learn, FAQ — sit between the last dark anchor and the final CTA. On a phone that is roughly four screens of scrolling with no ask. This is the single biggest sequencing risk on the page.
210 and 13 overlap Both answer the same unspoken question — "will anyone know?". In sequence the quotes read as a preamble to the FAQ rather than a section of their own. Recommended consolidation: quotes become a three-across band directly above the accordion, one section instead of two.
312 sits in the wrong place Learn is the only section whose success is measured by the visitor leaving the page. At position 12 it invites exit at the exact moment momentum should be rebuilding toward 14. Recommended: move it below the final CTA, above the legal band.
406 and 08 both explain Andropause explains the condition; clinical credibility explains the process. Neither is redundant, but with 05 and 07 between them the page spends four consecutive sections explaining. Consider whether 06 belongs closer to 04, where the visitor is still identifying his problem.
503 is conditional, and 04 must not know it Most first-time visitors never see section 03, because they have not completed the assessment. The page must read correctly with 03 absent — meaning 04 has to follow 02 without a seam, and 07 must not be the first dark band a scroller meets after a long light run.
6Page length Sixteen sections is roughly 9,500px on desktop and 14,000px on a 390px phone. That is long but defensible if the trough is fixed — the assessment is at the top, so page length costs nothing to the visitor who converts early.
If you approve consolidation later The fourteen-section order would be: 00 nav · 01 hero · 02 assessment · 03 outcome · 04 care areas · 05 how it works · 06 andropause · 07 why SoviCare · 08 credibility · 09 longevity · 10 supplements · 11 proof + FAQ · 12 final CTA · 13 learn · 14 legal + footer. Two sections fewer, one continuous rise from 09 to the final CTA, and the exit-friendly section placed after the last ask rather than before it.

Main menu v3.

Option F, a classic dropdown with no sub-menu, reduced after the website review: Longevity and Hair loss left the site, and Help became a direct FAQ link.

Structure
SoviCare Sexual health ⌄How it worksFAQ Sign in Get started

Desktop

  • Sexual health opens a dropdown with Tadalafil, Sildenafil and SoviCare Trio, each with a thumbnail and one meta line, plus “View all”.
  • How it works and FAQ are direct links.
  • Sign in (text link) and Get started (primary button) on the right.

Mobile

  • Menu button opens a full-screen panel: Sexual health (accordion) · How it works · FAQ · Sign in.
  • Get started is fixed at the bottom with the line “Assessment only. Not a diagnosis.”
Behaviour

Motion and hover (brand hub tokens)

  • Nav item hover: underline grows left → right, 220ms ease.
  • Dropdown open/close, item hover and focus: timings and colours exactly as in the Figma interactions spec (motion tokens 140 / 180 / 220 / 320ms, one easing: ease).
  • Buttons: Primary #264653 → #1C3742; Outline fills Deep Teal; 180ms ease.
  • Focus-visible: 2px Blue outline, 3px offset. Full keyboard support (Enter/Space opens, Esc closes).
  • Sticky header. prefers-reduced-motion → instant.
